## App Context

### Core Philosophy
- Based on "Your Money or Your Life" by Vicki Robin
- Privacy-first: no accounts, localStorage + export/import
- Life energy concept: Express costs in hours of life, not just money
- Icelandic market focus (all UI text in Icelandic)

### Key UX Principles
1. **Plain Language Outputs**: "This costs you 14 work-hours per month" not just "$X"
2. **Annualized Numbers Everywhere**: Monthly → Yearly → Years-to-FI impact
3. **Assumption Transparency**: Every result shows what inputs drove it
4. **Privacy-First**: No account required; export/import local files
5. **Life Energy Units**: Goals and costs expressed in hours, not just dollars

- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS
- **Backend**: Node.js API routes (Vercel serverless)
- **State**: Local state + localStorage (privacy-first)

## FIRE Math Reference

### Key Formulas
1. **Net Annual Income** = Gross Income - Work Expenses
2. **Savings Rate** = (Net Annual Income - Annual Expenses) / Net Annual Income × 100
3. **FI Number** = Annual Expenses × FI Multiplier (typically 25x-30x)
4. **Years to FI** = Calculate using savings rate, current net worth, FI number, expected return

### Standard Assumptions
- **Expected Return Rate**: 7% (typical default, adjustable)
- **FI Multiplier**: 25x (4% rule) or 30x (3.33% rule)
- **Inflation**: Can be considered in future iterations
