# Feature Context: Compound Savings Life Energy Calculator

## App: peninganaedalifid (Icelandic FIRE Calculator)

### Technology Stack
- **Framework**: Next.js 16.1.3 with App Router
- **Language**: TypeScript 5
- **UI**: React 19, Tailwind CSS 4
- **Testing**: Vitest with Testing Library
- **State**: React Context (CalculatorContext)
- **Storage**: localStorage with export/import

### Core Concepts
1. **Life Energy**: Hours of work required to afford something (from "Your Money or Your Life")
2. **Actual Hourly Wage**: Calculated from gross income minus time/money expenses
3. **Privacy-First**: No accounts, all data in localStorage + JSON export/import
4. **Icelandic Context**: All UI in Icelandic, ISK currency, Icelandic savings rates

### Feature Location in APP_PLAN
- **Feature**: 2.2.3 Compound Savings Life Energy Calculator
- **Category**: Financial Independence Planning Tools
- **Priority**: Medium complexity
- **Dependencies**: Actual Hourly Wage from main calculator

### Feature Requirements
**User Need**: See how regular savings grow over time in life energy terms

**Inputs**:
- Monthly savings amount (ISK)
- Interest rate (%)
- Time horizon (years)

**Output**:
- Future value in ISK
- Equivalent life energy hours saved
- Compound growth visualization
- "Life energy earned" from interest alone

**Features**:
- Compare different savings rates
- Show impact of compound interest in life energy terms
- Icelandic context: typical savings rates, verðtryggð (inflation-indexed) options
