# Compound Savings Life Energy Calculator

## Location
`apps/peninganaedalifid/src/components/savings/CompoundSavingsCalculator.tsx`

## Purpose
Interactive calculator that visualizes how regular monthly savings grow over time with compound interest, displaying results in both Icelandic Kronur (ISK) and life energy hours. Based on "Your Money or Your Life" principles, it helps users understand the true long-term benefit of consistent saving habits.

## Exports
- `CompoundSavingsCalculator` - Main calculator component

## Key Functionality
- **Real-time Calculation**: Automatically calculates compound interest as users adjust inputs
- **Life Energy Conversion**: Converts future value and interest earned to hours of life energy
- **Icelandic Savings Presets**: Quick buttons for common Icelandic savings account types:
  - Verðtryggt (inflation-indexed): 3.0%
  - Venjulegur sparnaður (regular savings): 1.5%
  - Hávaxtasparnaður (high-yield savings): 2.5%
- **Three Result Cards**:
  1. Future Value: Total savings after time period (ISK + life energy)
  2. Interest Earned: "Free" life energy from compound growth (highlighted as "Gjöf!")
  3. Total Contributions: Amount actually saved over time
- **Missing Wage Prompt**: Alerts users when actualHourlyWage is not available

## Calculation Formula
Uses standard compound interest formula for annuity due (beginning-of-period payments):

```
FV = P × [((1 + r)^n - 1) / r] × (1 + r)
```

Where:
- P = monthly payment
- r = monthly interest rate (annual rate / 12)
- n = total number of months

## Notes
- Formula uses annuity due (beginning-of-period) which is standard for savings where deposits occur at the start of each month
- Interest earned life energy is highlighted as "Gjöf!" (gift) to emphasize that this is "free" life energy generated by money working for you
- Missing wage prompt is non-blocking - users can still use ISK-only mode without actualHourlyWage
