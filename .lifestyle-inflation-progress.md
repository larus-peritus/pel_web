## Key Design Decisions Made

1. **9 Spending Categories**: Balance between detail and simplicity
   - Enough to detect patterns, not overwhelming
   - Matches common personal finance categories
   - "Other" provides flexibility

2. **4-Level Scoring System**:
   - Healthy (0-5%), Caution (5-15%), Warning (15-30%), Critical (30%+)
   - Based on spending increase beyond income increase
   - Clear thresholds for actionable insights

3. **Icelandic-First**:
   - All UI text, labels, and suggestions in Icelandic
   - Follows existing app patterns
   - Month names, error messages, suggestions all localized

4. **Life Energy Integration**:
   - All calculations can work with or without actualHourlyWage
   - Gracefully degrades if wage not available
   - Shows both króna amounts and life energy hours

5. **FI Impact Focus**:
   - Every analysis shows FI delay impact
   - Future value calculations at 7% return
   - 4% rule (25× annual expenses)
   - Clear cost in years/months of delay
